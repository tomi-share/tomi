# Building and distributing your own version

You are free to fork Tomi (Apache-2.0). If you only build it for yourself, nothing below is required. If you
**distribute** your build to other people (a store, a website), change the identity first, so that it cannot be mistaken
for the official build and cannot collide with it on a device or in a store. The rules are in
[`TRADEMARKS.md`](../TRADEMARKS.md); this page lists where the identifiers live.

Change together:

- the **display name and icon** (see `design/tomi/brand-guidelines.md` for what belongs to the official identity);
- the **application identifier** of each platform (below);
- the **signing keys** and developer accounts (never reuse or commit the official ones; keys are CI secrets);
- the **relay**: clients take the relay from the pairing link, or from the optional build default `TOMI_RELAY_URL`.
  Do not point your build at someone else's relay without their agreement.

Everything else (the protocol, the frozen format in `spec/vectors/`) must stay as is for your clients to remain
interoperable.

## Android

The application id is a Gradle property. The Kotlin package (`namespace`) can stay `eu.shaffe.tomi`.

```sh
cd apps/android
./gradlew assembleRelease -PtomiApplicationId=com.example.myfork -PtomiRelayUrl=https://relay.example.org
```

Set the property in `gradle.properties` (or in the CI invocation) to make it permanent. The file provider authority
(`<applicationId>.attachments`) follows it. Also change `app_name` in `strings.xml`, the launcher icon, and sign with your
own keystore (no signing key is stored in this repository).

The `tomi-share://` URL scheme in `AndroidManifest.xml` is shared by every build that declares it: if your fork must be
installable next to the official app, change it too (and `DeepLinkParser`).

## Windows (Tauri)

- `apps/windows/src-tauri/tauri.conf.json`: `productName`, `identifier` (this also names the data directory under
  `%LOCALAPPDATA%`), the window titles and the installer settings.
- `apps/windows/src-tauri/src/notify.rs`: the `AUMID` constant (notifications), keep it equal to the identifier.
- Sign the installer with your own certificate (the official builds are unsigned development builds).

The DPAPI entropy label in `wrapkey.rs` is an internal cryptographic label, not an identity: leave it unless you know
why you change it (changing it makes existing wrapped keys unreadable).

## iOS (SwiftUI)

Generated by XcodeGen from `apps/apple/project.yml`. Change:

- `bundleIdPrefix` and the three `PRODUCT_BUNDLE_IDENTIFIER` values (app, share extension, tests), and `PRODUCT_NAME`;
- the **App Group** `group.eu.shaffe.tomi`: in `project.yml` (two targets) and in `Tomi/Tomi.entitlements` and
  `TomiShare/TomiShare.entitlements`, then register it in your Apple developer account;
- the background task identifiers `eu.shaffe.tomi.refresh` and `eu.shaffe.tomi.send`: in `Tomi/Info.plist`
  (`BGTaskSchedulerPermittedIdentifiers`) and in `Tomi/App/BackgroundWork.swift` (the two must match);
- the URL scheme in `Tomi/Info.plist` (`tomi-share`) if your fork must coexist with the official app;
- your own `DEVELOPMENT_TEAM`, provisioning and associated domains (`applinks:<your relay>`).

Other strings starting with `eu.shaffe.tomi` in the Swift sources (queue labels, notification categories, the
`UserDefaults` suite) are internal names and do not need to change.

## Checking your fork

Run the checks listed in [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](../CONTRIBUTING.md): the frozen vectors must stay unchanged, and the core
tests must pass.
